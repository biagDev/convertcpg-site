## Why a product page is an argument

A gallery, a price and a button cannot explain why a product is worth it. Conviction gives the page room to make the case: what it is, why it is different, the proof, and the answer to every doubt.

### The order matters

Claim, proof, objection, offer. Shoppers read in that order whether or not the page is built for it.

## What the Rail does

The Rail keeps Add to cart on screen and changes its message as the shopper reads. It is the one thing merchants ask about most.

> A buy button that knows what they just read.

## How to test your own page

1. Open your best-selling product on a phone.
2. Scroll once. Can you still buy?
3. Scroll again. Does the page answer the next question, or repeat the first one?

| Check | Typical theme | Conviction |
|---|---|---|
| Sticky buy button | Static | Message follows the section |
| Proof placement | Footer badges | Next to the claim |
| Bundles | Another app | Native tiers |
