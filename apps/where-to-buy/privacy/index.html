What the app stores,

Effective date: November 4, 2026. This policy covers the ConvertCPG Where to Buy app for Shopify ("the app"), made by The Mendolia Group Corp, doing business as ConvertCPG ("we", "us"). Contact: support@convertcpg.com.

It is written for two readers: the merchant who installs the app, and the shopper who uses a store finder on a merchant's website. The short version: the app stores the merchant's retailers, store addresses, product-to-retailer links and settings. It stores nothing about shoppers and nothing about the merchant's customers.

Who this policy is for#

Merchants. You install the app in your Shopify admin. We process your store's data to run the app for you. For that data, you are the controller and we are your processor, under the Shopify Partner Program Agreement and the terms you accept when you install the app.

Shoppers. You visit a merchant's website and use the store finder or see "Find it at" on a product page. The app answers your search and forgets it. We do not know who you are.

What the app stores about a merchant#

When you install the app, we store:

  • Your store's .myshopify.com domain and the access token Shopify issues to the app, so the app can read your products and themes and write the data below to your store. Tokens are stored as Shopify issues them, in a database on an encrypted volume.
  • The retailers and stores you add: retailer name, website, buy-online link, logo image address, and for each store its name, street address, city, region, postal code, country, store phone, map coordinates and your own tags. These are business records of retail stores. The app never asks for a person's name.
  • Which of your products each retailer sells, and any store-level product lists.
  • Your app settings, including your own Google Maps browser key if you choose Google Maps.
  • Your upload history: file names, row counts, status, and a copy of the rows that had problems so you can download them. The uploaded file itself is kept only while the upload runs, then removed.
  • Which themes we checked for the app's sections and the result.
  • The name, domain and contact email of your store, read from Shopify, so our support chat can greet you and reply to you.

We do not request access to your customers, orders, checkouts or any other customer data, and we hold none. The app has no scope that could read it.

What the app writes to your Shopify store#

The app keeps the source of truth in your own store, where you can see and edit it:

  • Retailers and stores as metaobjects (Content > Metaobjects).
  • A product metafield listing the retailers that sell each product.
  • App settings as app-owned metafields on the app installation.
  • One page called "Where to buy", only if you click the setup button and grant the optional page permission. The page is yours and stays after you remove the app.

Our server keeps a mirror of the retailers, stores and product links for fast search.

What the app knows about a shopper#

Nothing that identifies you.

  • When you search, your typed ZIP code or city, or your coordinates rounded to about one kilometer if you click "Use my location", travel to our server, are used to find the closest stores, and are dropped. They are not stored and not written to our logs.
  • The app sets no cookies and uses no browser storage on the merchant's website. It loads no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • The app never reads your IP address. Like every web server, our hosting provider sees the address of each request, and the map service sees it when your browser loads map tiles (see below).
  • "Use my location" asks your browser's permission every time and remembers nothing between visits.
  • "Directions" opens Google Maps with the store's address only when you click it.

Crisp (France) Merchant side only, never on the storefront. We use no other service. The app sends no email. It has no analytics, advertising or error-tracking provider. Its only outgoing connections are to Shopify, Geocodio and, through the shopper's browser, the map provider.

Logs#

Our server writes plain log lines to our hosting provider's log stream and nowhere else. A line can hold a request's method, path, status, size and timing with no query text; a merchant's store domain with a webhook name, job counts or an error message; a theme id; a plan name; or the performance numbers Shopify sends, with a two-letter country code and no identifiers. A line never holds a shopper's search, coordinates or IP address, and never a secret. The provider keeps the stream for a short period, typically days, after which it is gone. We keep no copies.

How long we keep data, and how it is deleted#

  • While the app is installed: the data above, for as long as you use the app. You can edit or delete retailers, stores and product links any time in the app or in Content > Metaobjects.
  • When you remove the app: the access token is deleted at once. Shopify tells us 48 hours later to delete everything else, and we do: every row for your store, in one step, with the counts logged. Your metaobjects, the product metafield and the Where to buy page stay in your Shopify store; they are yours.
  • If you reinstall: Shopify keeps the app's metaobjects for a while and reconnects them. The app copies them back so your stores return without a new upload.
  • Address lookups: we cache lookup results shared across stores as a hash of the address text and its coordinates, with no store or merchant attached. Results expire after 365 days (addresses), 90 days (places) or 7 days (not found).
  • Support chats: kept in our Crisp account under Crisp's terms. Email support@convertcpg.com to have a conversation deleted.
  • Customer data requests from Shopify: the app answers them and has nothing to return or delete, because it holds no customer data.

Your rights#

Merchants can see and change their data in the app and in their own Shopify admin at any time, and can remove the app to delete our copy. For a copy of the data we hold for your store, a correction, or deletion before the 48-hour window, email support@convertcpg.com from your store's contact address. We answer within one business day.

Shoppers: we hold no data about you, so there is nothing to look up or delete. If you believe otherwise, write to the same address and we will check.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the merchant whose site you visited is the controller of any data about you; our processing happens in the United States under the merchant's instructions.

Security#

Connections to the app use TLS. Access tokens and the database live on our hosting provider's encrypted volumes. Only the founder has access to the server and the database, with two-factor authentication on every account involved. The app is checked with Shopify's app security scan before every release.

Changes#

If we change what the app stores or who receives it, we update this page and the effective date. Material changes are announced inside the app.

Contact#

The Mendolia Group Corp, doing business as ConvertCPG. support@convertcpg.com. We answer within one business day, Monday to Friday, US Eastern time.
