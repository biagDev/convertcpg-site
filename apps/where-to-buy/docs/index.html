What the app does#

ConvertCPG Where to Buy shows shoppers which stores near them sell your products.

  • The store finder is a section you add to a page, usually a page called "Where to buy". Shoppers type a ZIP code or city, or click "Use my location", and see the closest stores in a list and on a map.
  • "Find it at" is a section on your product pages. It lists the retailers that sell that product, with their logos.
  • You add your retailers and stores once. You pick which products each retailer sells once. Every store of that retailer then shows those products.

No map account and no key to set up. The map is included.

Setup, in order#

Open the app from Apps in your Shopify admin. App Home shows a setup guide with five steps. Do them in order.

1. Add your stores#

Upload your store list and we add the retailers for you. Or add one retailer and its stores by hand.

Upload: go to Upload stores. Click Download a sample file to get a spreadsheet you can fill in. Save it as a CSV file and drop it on the page. We check every row first and tell you what we found. Nothing is saved until you click Save stores. Then we put each store on the map from its address, usually within a minute. Large lists take a bit longer. You can leave the page; the upload keeps going. The CSV format is below.

By hand: go to Retailers > Add a retailer, then add its stores. A retailer is the chain that owns stores, like Target or Whole Foods. A store with no chain is an independent store. This step is done once you have at least one store; a retailer with no stores does not finish it.

For each retailer you can set a website and a "Buy online link" (a link to your products on their site). To add a logo, go to Content > Metaobjects > Retailer in your Shopify admin, open the retailer, and upload an image. Logos show in "Find it at" and in the store finder.

2. Pick where each product is sold#

Go to Products. Every product in your store is listed. Check the box for each retailer that sells it. Click Save.

Shoppers then see those retailers in "Find it at" on that product's page, and the store finder's product filter shows the stores that carry it.

The checkbox under a retailer's name checks or clears every product on the page at once.

3. Show "Find it at" on your product pages#

On App Home, click Add to product pages. Your theme editor opens on a product page with "Find it at" already added under the product information. Click Save in the top right.

If it lands at the bottom of the page, drag it under your Buy buttons in the editor, then Save.

4. Set up your Where to buy page#

On App Home, click Set up my Where to buy page. Shopify asks you to let the app make a page. Say yes, and the app makes a page called "Where to buy" and opens your theme editor with the store finder added. Click Save.

The editor opens on your Where to buy page. At the top it says "Default page": that is the layout all your pages share. Shoppers only see the store finder on your Where to buy page.

Prefer to do it yourself? Make a page called Where to buy (leave the text empty), then in the theme editor add the store finder to it and click Save. Named your page something else? Click the store finder in the editor and pick your page under "Show on this page".

5. Look at a product page#

Open a product that is sold in stores. You should see "Find it at" with its retailers. Open your Where to buy page and search a ZIP code.

The CSV file#

One store per row. Column names in the first row. Up to 5,000 stores per file; split bigger lists into several files. Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets all work: save or download as CSV. If a value has a comma in it, put quotes around it.

Column Must have? What to put in it Example
store_name Yes The store name shoppers see Sprouts Farmers Market #412
retailer No The chain. Leave empty for an independent store Sprouts
address1 Yes Street address 1111 W 6th St
address2 No Suite, unit or mall Suite 100
city Yes City Austin
region No State or province TX or Texas
postal_code Yes ZIP or postal code 78703, M5E 1Z2
country Yes 2-letter code or the country name US, CA, MX
phone No The store's phone 512-555-0100
latitude, longitude No Leave empty. We find the store on the map for you
products No Only if this one store sells different products than its retailer. Product URL handles with ; between them original-12oz;mango-12oz
tags No Your own labels, ; between them grocery;flagship

We also understand common other names: name, store, chain, banner, address, street, state, province, zip, zipcode, postcode, country_code, lat, lng, lon, product_handles, tag. Column order and upper or lower case do not matter.

Sample file (the same one the app gives you):

store_name,retailer,address1,address2,city,region,postal_code,country,phone,latitude,longitude,products,tags
Sprouts Farmers Market #412,Sprouts,1111 W 6th St,,Austin,TX,78703,US,512-555-0100,,,,grocery
Sprouts Farmers Market #380,Sprouts,4600 W Lovers Ln,,Dallas,TX,75209,US,214-555-0101,,,,grocery
Whole Foods Market Lamar,Whole Foods Market,525 N Lamar Blvd,,Austin,TX,78703,US,512-555-0102,30.2706,-97.7530,,grocery;flagship
Target T-1234,Target,"5406 Brodie Ln, Suite 100",,Sunset Valley,TX,78745,US,,,,original-12oz;mango-12oz,mass

Putting stores on the map. Leave latitude and longitude empty for stores in the US, Canada and Mexico. For a store in another country, fill in both, or enter its exact spot on the Stores page after the upload (in Google Maps, right-click the store and copy the numbers at the top).

Uploading again. Upload the same list any time. We match stores by retailer, street address and ZIP code. Same rows: nothing changes. Changed name or phone: updated. New rows: added.

What we check. Rows with a problem are marked "Will be skipped" with the reason; rows we save but want you to look at are "Will be saved". Click Download rows with problems to get just those rows with a column that says what to fix. Common ones: a missing street or ZIP, a ZIP that lost its first zero (we fix it and tell you), a retailer spelled almost like one you have ("Sprout" for "Sprouts": fix the spelling, or the stores would show none of that retailer's products), a product handle that is not in your store, the same store twice.

Retailers and a store's own list#

Products are picked per retailer. Every store of that retailer shows them.

If one store sells a different set, give that store its own list: the products column in the file, or on the Products page under "Different at some stores". A store's own list replaces the retailer's list for that store only. "Find it at" on the product page always shows retailers, not single stores.

The two sections and where they go#

Both are app blocks you place in your theme editor (Online Store > Themes > Customize). The app never changes your theme's code.

Store finder. Goes on a page. Shoppers only see it on the page you choose (your Where to buy page by default). On Dawn and most themes, the editor puts it in an Apps section on the page. On Horizon and other newer themes it works the same way. In the editor you can pick its page under "Show on this page".

"Find it at". Goes on product pages only. On Dawn the app adds it inside the product information section, under the price and buy buttons. On themes whose product section has a different name, the app finds the right section for you; if it lands at the bottom of the page, drag it under your Buy buttons and Save. It shows nothing for a product with no retailers picked, so it is safe to leave on every product page.

Settings#

App Settings (Settings in the left menu) set the defaults for your store finder:

  • Show distances in miles or kilometers.
  • How far to search at first: 10, 25, 50 or 100.
  • What shoppers see first: a map and a list (recommended), or only a list.
  • Which map to use: our map (free, nothing to set up) or Google Maps with your own Google key.

In the theme editor, each store finder can differ from the app's settings: layout (list left and map right, or map on top), map height, corner roundness, accent color (markers, store numbers, the picked store; empty uses your theme's text color), map look (match the page, light, dark), distance unit, how far to search, whether shoppers can pick a retailer, a product filter (pick a collection), how many stores to list before a search (10 to 50), a heading, and the text to show before you have stores.

"Find it at" has its own settings: heading (default "Find it in stores at"), show logos, names or both, what happens when a shopper clicks a retailer (open the store finder page, open the retailer's website, or nothing), which page is your store finder, how many retailers to show, and the "Find a store near me" link.

Advanced (in Settings, collapsed). Most stores never need it. It holds the store finder web address. Only change it if you changed the app's web address in Shopify (Settings > Apps > ConvertCPG Where to Buy).

How the map looks#

The default map uses OpenFreeMap, a free map that needs no key and no account. It loads after the rest of your page, only when it is near the screen, so your page stays fast. Light pages get a light map, dark pages a dark one, or you choose. Stores are numbered to match the list; nearby stores group into a circle with a count that zooms in when clicked. There is a full screen button and zoom buttons. Each store card has a Directions link that opens Google Maps.

Google Maps with your own key. In Settings, choose Google Maps and paste a browser key from Google Cloud. Limit the key to your store's web address and the Maps JavaScript API. Shoppers' browsers use your key each time the store finder page opens, Google bills you for map use, and most stores stay inside Google's free monthly amount. The key is visible in your page code, which is normal for Google browser keys. We never use your key ourselves.

Switching themes#

Your sections are added to a theme, not to your store. When you publish a different theme, they are not on it yet.

App Home watches for this. If your live theme is missing the store finder or "Find it at", App Home shows a warning with a button that opens your theme editor with the section added. Click Save. You can also check a theme you are getting ready before you publish it: "Your theme" card > Check a theme.

To do it by hand on a new theme: run steps 3 and 4 again.

Troubleshooting#

A store says "Adding to map" for a long time. We place stores from their addresses, up to a daily amount. Big uploads can take until the next day. The store shows in the list meanwhile.

A store says "Fix address". We could not find the address. Check the street and ZIP code on the Stores page, or enter the exact spot (latitude and longitude) yourself.

A store says "Check location". We found it, but maybe not in the exact spot. Open it and look at the map. If it is wrong, fix the address or enter the exact spot.

The store finder says "Search isn't working right now". The list still shows. Usually it passes in a minute. If it stays, open Settings > Advanced and click "Check this address"; if you changed the app's web address in Shopify, save the one Shopify shows.

"The map didn't load" or "The map images didn't load". The list still works. Reload the page. If it stays, email us.

"Find it at" is empty on a product. Go to Products and check the retailers that sell it, then Save. It shows nothing on purpose when no retailer is picked.

I published a new theme and the sections are gone. See Switching themes above. App Home has the one-click fix.

My upload was refused because of my plan. Remove rows, or upgrade. Shoppers still see all your stores.

FAQ#

Do I need a Google Maps key? No. The map is included. Google is an option if you prefer it.

Do you store anything about my shoppers? No. A shopper's search is answered and forgotten. Nothing about them is stored or logged. See the privacy policy.

Where is my data? Your retailers and stores are saved in your own Shopify store (Content > Metaobjects), plus a copy on our server for fast search. Removing the app removes our copy within 48 hours; your Shopify records stay yours.

Can I rename the Where to buy page? Yes. Change the title in Online Store > Pages. If you use a different page, pick it in the theme editor under "Show on this page".

Can I show different products at one store? Yes: give that store its own list (above).

Which countries work? The map works everywhere. We place stores from their addresses in the US, Canada and Mexico; for other countries, enter latitude and longitude.

I removed the app and my theme still lists the store finder or "Find it at" as missing. Shopify can keep an entry for a removed app's section in a theme, usually on a theme that is not live. Open your theme editor, click the missing entry, and click Remove block. To avoid it next time, take the two sections out of your theme first, then remove the app.

I reinstalled the app. Where are my stores? They come back by themselves. Your retailers and stores were kept in your Shopify store, and App Home shows "Restoring your stores from Shopify" while the app copies them back. A few thousand stores take a minute or two. Products you had marked as sold in stores come back too. If the page says it couldn't finish, click Try again.

How fast do you answer support? Within one business day. Chat with us from App Home, or email support@convertcpg.com.
