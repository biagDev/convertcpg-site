Get help with ConvertCPG Where to Buy#

Questions, or something not working? We are glad to help.

Chat with us. Open the app from Apps in your Shopify admin and click Chat with us on App Home or Settings. You reach the people who built the app.

Email us. support@convertcpg.com

We answer within one business day. Business days are Monday to Friday, US Eastern time. Most messages get an answer faster than that.

What to put in your message#

The more of this you include, the faster we can fix it:

  1. Your store's web address (the one that ends in .myshopify.com is best).
  2. What you were trying to do, and what happened instead. The exact words of any message you saw help a lot.
  3. Where it happened: the app (which page), the theme editor, or your storefront (which page).
  4. A screenshot or a short screen recording, if you can.
  5. For an upload problem: the name of your file, and if you can, the file itself or the "rows with problems" download.
  6. For a map problem: one store name and its address.

We never need your Shopify password, and we will never ask for it.

Before you write, two quick checks#

  • A section is missing after a theme change. Open the app's App Home. If your live theme is missing the store finder or "Find it at", you will see a warning with a one-click fix.
  • Search says it isn't working. Wait a minute and try again. If it stays, open Settings > Advanced in the app and click "Check this address".

The help docs cover setup, the CSV file, settings, plans and common fixes: convertcpg.com/apps/where-to-buy/docs

Privacy#

What the app stores and with whom it shares it: convertcpg.com/apps/where-to-buy/privacy. To ask for a copy or deletion of your data, email support@convertcpg.com from your store's contact address.
